# Components

The components the storefront ships, grouped by where they live in `apps/store/src/`. A **drawn** component's look comes from its set in `~/designs/proteus/Proteus.figcraft`, exported to the `<name>.variants.ts` beside it; a **hand-written** one has no set and composes drawn parts or renders nothing styled of its own. Colours are `color/*` roles, text is `type/*` (`DESIGN.md`).

## From FigCraft to code

**The page decides the folder.** Each component has a folder of its own holding the hand-written component and, beside it, its generated variants file:

| FigCraft page | Code folder |
|---|---|
| `components/ui` | `src/components/ui/<name>/` (store primitives, on `@base-ui/react`) |
| `components/store` | `src/components/store/<name>/` (store-wide blocks: header, footer, form fields, panel, pagination) |
| `entities/<entity>` | `src/entities/<entity>/ui/<name>/` |
| `features/<feature>` | `src/features/<feature>/components/<name>/` |
| `app/<group>` | none: screen sets are composed by hand in the routes from the parts above |

**Generated, never edited by hand** (each exporter in `@proteus/design` has a `--check` in the verify gate):

- `<name>.variants.ts` from `components:export <Name>`: one `cva()` per styled layer, with Tailwind classes. The component set is `<Name>`; layer names are the `cva` export names (`button` for the component, `buttonLabel` for its layer named `label`). Snapshot: `packages/design/figcraft/components/<Name>.json`.
- `src/components/ui/icon/icon.glyphs.tsx` from `icons:export`: one entry per variant of the `Icon` set (`name=<kebab-case>`, one colour, 24 × 24), the inside of the `<svg>` drawn in `currentColor`, and the `IconName` type. The hand-written `icon.tsx` beside it draws the frame, so an icon is a typed `name` prop, not a `lucide-react` import. The glyph file is generated; `icon.tsx` is hand-written. Snapshot: `packages/design/figcraft/icons/<name>.svg`.
- Brand images from `brand:export`, into `apps/store/public/`: each frame of the `brand` page's `export` section is the file its pluginData names there (`docs/design/brand-marks.md`). `brand:check` fails when a recorded file is missing, resized or edited since the export. Snapshot: `packages/design/figcraft/brand.json`.
- `src/navigation/flow.gen.ts` from `flow:export`: per screen, per event, the transitions drawn on the `flows` page, same-screen events included (they change the URL). Snapshot: `packages/design/figcraft/flows.json`.
- `DESIGN.md`'s frontmatter from `design-md`: colors, typography, rounded and spacing from the token snapshot; components from the component snapshots, one entry per exported set variant.
- The theme (`packages/design/tokens/tokens.gen.css`) from `tokens:export`.

**Hand-written:**

- The component's `.tsx` beside its variants: the `@base-ui/react` part, props, ARIA, focus and keyboard, text as children or props, and the state the design can't draw (open, `aria-invalid`, data). It imports the variants and `cn()` from `@proteus/ui-core`. Its only own classes are the ones no design property holds, each for a reason:
  - focus and pointer mechanics: `outline-none`, `disabled:pointer-events-none`, `disabled:cursor-not-allowed`, `select-none`, `cursor-*`, and `FloatingLabelSelect`'s `aria-invalid:` ring (a validation state, not an interaction);
  - the press offset, `active:not-aria-[haspopup]:translate-y-px` on `Button` (and `AlertDialog`'s buttons): it moves the component itself, and a variant's place in its set is the set's layout, not a property;
  - an inset focus outline, `-outline-offset-2 focus-visible:outline focus-visible:outline-ink` (`peer-focus-visible:` on `TextValue` and `SwatchValue`), on `QuantityStepper`, `GalleryDot` and the `ProductGallery` track, and `SavedCardRow`'s remove button's `focus-visible:outline-2`: FigCraft has no stroke offset;
  - where Base UI places a part: the `Tooltip` and `Popover` arrows' side and offset (`data-[side=top]:-bottom-3.75`, the translate), on `components.runtimePositions` and `components.positionerAnchored` so a preview position never reaches code;
  - line wrapping, which FigCraft has no property for: `text-balance` on the `NotFound` and `AlertDialog` descriptions, `md:text-pretty` on the latter;
  - motion: `transition-*`, the drawer, toast and popup transitions (`data-open:` / `data-closed:` fades, zooms and slides) and the `animate-*` keyframes (accordion height in `src/primitives.css`, tw-animate, skeleton pulse);
  - a box whose height follows its width or data: the `aspect-4/5` photographs (product cards, gallery slides, swatches, bag, summary and order rows, their skeletons) and fractional skeleton widths, on `components.runtimeSizes`; their photographs are data (`components.runtimeFills`);
  - box behaviour: `shrink-0`, `whitespace-nowrap`, `bg-clip-padding`, `min-w-0`, `min-h-0`, `appearance-none`, the `Checkbox` and `RadioGroupItem` hit area (`after:absolute after:-inset-x-3 after:-inset-y-2`), and sizing the caller's `<svg>` (`[&_svg]:size-4`; `Button › icon` is on `components.notStyledLayers`, so the icon slot exports no cva).
- Screen composition in routes and feature components, and the prose of `DESIGN.md` and this file.
- The screen table, `src/navigation/screens.ts`: each design screen (`ProductDetail`), and each state with a route or URL of its own (`AddressBook/addressEdit`, `Root/search`), to the route that renders it and where a link to it goes. `Root` is the `_main` chrome; `AuthLayout` and `CheckoutLayout` the chrome of those layouts; drawers are states of the screen they cover.

**Navigation.** `src/navigation/` may be imported only by routes (`navigation-only-from-routes` in `structure/.dependency-cruiser.cjs`), so `flowLinks` is called only there. A route calls `flowLinks('<Screen>')` (`src/navigation/links.ts`) and passes the events it needs to its feature as props typed `LinkTarget` (`src/lib/link-target.ts`), or functions returning one; a feature that takes links declares them in its `types/links.ts`. Below the routes a `LinkTarget` is opaque: the component spreads it on `<Link>`, so every navigation is a real `<a href>` with preloading, also during SSR, or passes it to `navigate` after a mutation, and never builds one. An event with several targets takes the target first (`links.SIGNED_IN('AccountDetail')`). An event drawn to `./state` (filter, sort, page) stays on the current route, whichever renders the screen: its function takes the search params to change and returns `{ to: '.', search }` with them merged over the current ones, so one feature (the product list) serves several routes. `flow:check` (`check:flow`) fails when a target on the map has no entry in the screen table, a screen on the map has no route, a route is no screen's, or a leaf route doesn't render its screen (itself or through its layout). Routes that render only `<Outlet />`, or redirect with no component, are skipped by their shape.

**The viewport rule.** A set whose layout changes across breakpoints has a design-only `viewport` property with values `mobile`, `sm`, `md` and `lg` (Tailwind's `sm` 40rem, `md` 48rem, `lg` 64rem). It never becomes a `cva` prop. `mobile` values are the base classes; each larger value emits only the classes that differ from the next value down, under its prefix. `sm` and `md` are drawn only where the set changes there. Responsive behaviour is CSS only.

**The interaction rule.** A set whose look changes on hover, focus, press, when disabled or while its popup is open has a design-only `interaction` property with values `rest`, `hover`, `focus-visible`, `active`, `disabled` and `open` (a trigger whose popup shows, exported under `aria-expanded:`); only `rest` and the states that look different are drawn. It never becomes a `cva` prop. `rest` values are the base classes; each other value emits only the classes that differ from `rest`, under `hover:`, `focus-visible:`, `active:`, `disabled:` or `aria-expanded:` (a layer inside the component under `group-hover/<set>:` and the like, the root carrying `group/<set>`). With `viewport` the prefixes compose (`md:hover:`). A Base UI part that marks a state with a data attribute maps it in `design.json` (`components.interactionPrefixes`). A layer that is its own element with its own states (a stepper's buttons) is on `components.interactionParts`: it takes the plain variants and its layers follow it. A design-only `position` property (`default`, `first`, `last`, or a mapped value such as `afterCurrent`) works the same way for rules that depend on where an item sits in its list.

**Drawn, not hand-written:** `inline-flex` on `Button` (`components.inline` in `design.json`), `w-full` on the form controls (`components.fullWidth`: `Input`, `NativeSelect`, `Field`, `RadioGroup`), the transparent border every `Button` variant carries, the stretch of a vertical `Field`'s children (`items-stretch`), `RadioGroup`'s grid, opacity (`disabled:opacity-50`), a glyph's colour (`text-*` on the slot that holds it), the focus rings (`focus-visible:ring-3 focus-visible:ring-focus-ring` on `Button`, `Checkbox`, `RadioGroupItem`, and with `focus-visible:border-outline` on the `Accordion` trigger, `RouteDrawer` and `Toast` close buttons and `AlertDialog`'s cancel; the destructive action's `border-sale/40 ring-sale-ring`), `AlertDialog`'s `ring-1 ring-popup-ring`, the `shadow-lg` / `shadow-xl` of `Toast` and `DropdownMenu`, the alert dialog scrim's `backdrop-blur-xs`, `Tooltip`'s `max-w-56`, `RouteDrawer`'s scrolling body (`overflow-y-auto`), the alert dialog footer's mobile order (`flex-col-reverse sm:flex-row`), the drawer and popover panels' inherited `text-sm` (`type-body-compact`, `components.inheritedText`), `DropdownMenuItem`'s positional rules (`first:` and `[[data-current]+&]:border-transparent`), the footer accordion's closing `last:border-b`, and the per-part states of `QuantityStepper`, `Accordion`, `Pagination`, `Nav`, `RailPanel`, `SideMenu`, `ProductFilters`, `AlertDialog`, `RouteDrawer`, `Toast`, `FloatingLabelInput` and `SavedCardRemoveConfirm` (`components.interactionParts`).

**What is never exported:** screen sets (`app/*` pages, description starting "Screen set"), the `flows` page, the `brand` page's sources and previews, and design-only sets on the exporter's not-exported list.

**A component never fills its parent**, apart from the form controls on `components.fullWidth`. Width is the caller's layout (`className="w-full"`, fill width on the FigCraft instance). Callers pass layout classes only.


**Storybook.** `pnpm --filter store run storybook` (config in `apps/store/.storybook/`). Each primitive in `src/components/ui/` with a set has a `<name>.stories.tsx` beside it whose default args and control options come from its set's snapshot, imported from `@proteus/design/components/<Set>.json` and read with `setDefault` / `setOptions` (`@proteus/design/figcraft-set`), so the controls offer exactly what the design file draws. `Icon` has a story of all glyphs.

## src/components/ui

Store primitives on `@base-ui/react`, page `components/ui`, one folder each.

**Drawn:** `Button` and `ButtonLink` (`variant` default/outline/ghost/link/muted × `size` default/sm/icon/lg, `interaction` hover/focus-visible/disabled/open; `label`), `Input` (`invalid`, `interaction` focus-visible/disabled, `viewport` md; `value`), `Label`, `FieldLabel`, `FieldError` (`message`), `Field` (`orientation` vertical/horizontal; with `FieldSet` and `FieldGroup`), `Checkbox` and `RadioGroupItem` (`checked`, `interaction` focus-visible/disabled), `RadioGroup`, `NativeSelect` and `NativeSelectOption` (`value`), `Skeleton`, `Tooltip` (`label`), `Popover` (`size` default/rail; slot `content`; its arrow on `components.positionerAnchored`, its tip hand-written), `QuantityStepper` (`size` lg/xs × `variant` boxed/bare, `interaction` hover/disabled per button; `value`), `Toast` (`interaction` focus-visible on itself and its close; `title`, `description`, `hasDescription`; rendered by `Toaster` over `@proteus/ui-core`'s toast manager), `DropdownMenu` (slot `items`) and `DropdownMenuItem` (`current`, `highlighted`, `position` first/afterCurrent; `label`), `AlertDialog` (`variant` destructive/default, `viewport` mobile/sm, `interaction` focus-visible on action and cancel; `title`, `description`, `confirmText`, `cancelText`) and `AlertDialogOverlay`, `Drawer` (`side` right/left/up, `viewport` mobile/lg) with `DrawerOverlay` and `DrawerTitle` (`label`), `RouteDrawer` (`viewport` mobile/sm, `interaction` focus-visible on close; `title`, slots `body`, `footer`), `Accordion` (`variant` menu/footer/section, `position` last, `interaction` focus-visible on the trigger; `label`).

**Icon:** `Icon` (`src/components/ui/icon/`), the `Icon` set on `components/ui`, one variant per glyph (`name=<kebab-case>`), exported by `icons:export` into `icon.glyphs.tsx`; the hand-written `icon.tsx` draws the 24 × 24 `<svg>` frame in `currentColor` and takes `name: IconName`. Every glyph in the store is an `<Icon name="…" />`.

**Hand-written:** `Collapsible` (no classes of its own; its look is `CheckoutSummary`'s), `usePrompt` (`prompt/`: `@proteus/ui-core`'s `createPromptHook` bound to the store's `AlertDialog`).
